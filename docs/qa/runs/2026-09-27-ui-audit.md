## Implementation integrity verdict

**Pass.** The system is specific to this product rather than interchangeable. Tokens are named for what they mean (`--accent` is the annotation pencil, `--saved-text` is what the reader kept) and both surfaces draw from one set. Components carry product truth: a quote block shows its source and its Return control; a page card holds its own passages. The bundled detector reports 22 findings, all of them the false positive recorded in decision 30.

## Executive summary

- **17/20, Good.** Six issues found. P0: none. P1: none. P2: four. P3: two.
- All six were fixed in the same commit as this report, which departs from the playbook's "document, don't fix" rule because the fixes were one-line and verified in the same session.
- The honest gap is not in the code. Screen reader, high contrast, physical 200% zoom, and touch hardware remain untested, and no amount of static analysis substitutes.

## Findings

**P2 — Three disclosure controls were below the WCAG 2.2 target size (2.5.8, AA).** Measured: "How ReadTrail works" 252x17, "Import, export, and clear" 252x16 in the panel and 494x16 on the Desk. A bare `<summary>` renders at about 16px tall, so only the words were clickable. Fixed with padding and a 24px floor; re-measured at 25, 24 and 24.

**P2 — Token drift from this session's redesign.** `color: #1a1a2e` survived on accent buttons: the *previous* surface colour, left behind when the ground moved to `#17141f`. Every accent button label carried a blue cast on a warm palette. Now `var(--surface)`.

**P2 — Functional labels at 10px** (found by the detector, fixed in the previous commit). Six of the seven were a regression introduced earlier in the same session.

**P2 — Danger hover failed AA** (found by the detector, fixed in the previous commit). White on `#c05b55` measured 4.31:1.

**P3 — Return moved focus to a status line with no visible indicator.** A screen reader announces it, but a sighted keyboard reader saw nothing move, because `:focus-visible` does not match a programmatic focus. The status line now takes a visible ring on `:focus`.

**P3 — Two colour literals remain outside the token set**, both deliberate neutrals: `#ffffff` on the toggle knob and on the danger button label. A gradient endpoint on the Desk is also literal. Left as is; they carry no theme meaning.

## Verified healthy

- **Contrast**, computed directly against `--surface`: text 16.15, muted 7.36, accent 5.36, saved 11.27, error 10.15. Lowest is 5.36 against a 4.5 bar.
- **Accessible names**: every button, link, input, select, textarea and summary resolves a name under browser rules. An earlier probe of ours reported six unnamed controls; that probe did not follow `<label for>` and was wrong.
- **Focus**: a real Tab produces `solid 2px #8cc8ff`.
- **No horizontal overflow** at 280, 360, 400 or 1280px.
- **Heading order** is valid on both surfaces, including when the Desk stacks.
- **Animation** touches only `opacity` and `transform`; no `will-change` anywhere; reduced motion disables animation, transition and scroll behaviour.

## Not tested, and not claimed

NVDA or Narrator, Windows high contrast, physical 200% browser zoom, touch hardware, Chrome restart persistence, incognito window, and real third-party articles. These stay on the manual release checklist.

## Note on the tool

`npx impeccable install` fails at its skill-bundle download with HTTP 404 (its issue #479). `npx skills add pbakaus/impeccable` installs the same skill from GitHub and works. The installed files live in `.agents/` and are git-ignored; `skills-lock.json` pins the version so the exact skill can be reinstalled. The detector found two real faults the human eye had missed, and the audit playbook found four more. Its contrast rule, however, cannot read `color-scheme: dark` and must not be followed blindly (decision 30).
