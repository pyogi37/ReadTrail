## What happened in this session

A product reset. Four concepts were generated independently and judged through three lenses. The recommendation is in `docs/PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md` and the decisions are `docs/DECISIONS.md` items 20 to 26.

**The short version.** A clip stays under the page it came from, so capture never asks the reader to file anything. The reader's thinking lives in a **draft**: an ordered list of their own text blocks and quote blocks, where a quote points at a clip. Two sources are related because the reader quoted both in one draft. **Return** takes any quote back to its exact paragraph and says honestly how well it found it.

A trail-centric model was drafted first and rejected. A "current trail" the reader switches is a mode, and a wrong mode silently misfiles a week of clips.

## Two things not to undo

- The worker never sees a tab's URL (DECISIONS 14), which is why Return uses the session `seen` record rather than asking every tab where it is.
- An extension page open in a tab carries `sender.tab`, so the extension-origin check must stay first in `resolveTabId` and in the library handlers (DECISIONS 15).
