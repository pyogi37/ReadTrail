How ReadTrail works
  1. Turn it on for a page. Nothing is tracked until you do. While on, clicks pause or resume your reading line instead of opening links.
  2. Your place is temporary. It survives reloads in this browser session and disappears when you close Chrome.
  3. Save for later keeps it. Only pages you save appear below, with their exact URL and title stored on this device.
